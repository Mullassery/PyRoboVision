# PyRoboVision - Use Cases

## Overview

**Tracking and trajectory prediction that runs on CPU — with a clean boundary to NVIDIA's Alpamayo stack.** Pure Python (NumPy + SciPy) multi-object tracking, occlusion handling, and motion forecasting for robotics. > **Why it exists:** most robot stacks need identity persistence and short-horizon motion prediction long before they need another foundation model. PyRoboVision gives you a tested Kalman + Hungarian tracker, a trajectory predictor with uncertainty, and adapters for the models you already run — without dragging PyTorch into your perception core.
